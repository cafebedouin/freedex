A borrower locks collateral and names the principal, the repayment and the term; a lender funds it; the borrower repays before maturity or the lender takes the collateral after. The contract pays a 0.5% developer fee to SigmaFi's author on every loan, which the page shows and cannot remove; the contract's 0.4% "interface fee" is paid to the lender's own address here.

Offer tokens to a buyer

A clocked box: a box anyone may act on for you once per interval, one slice at a time, against the token's deepest ErgoDEX pool, with no server and no key but yours. Buy locks ERG and buys a slice of tokens per leg, the tokens to your address (dollar-cost averaging); sell locks tokens and sells a slice per leg, the ERG to your address (a tranche sale). Each leg must deliver at least the pool's own rule for the slice (less your tolerance) and may pay at most the executor fee you set to whoever runs it; a price cap (buy) or floor (sell) makes the program wait while the pool is dearer or cheaper. What the box locks beyond the legs differs by side: a buy program holds no storage-rent reserve, because a rent claim on a box left for four years takes its fee from the ERG budget and leaves a smaller program; a sell program holds one rent fee for its bytes, because a token box that cannot pay rent is consumed whole, tokens included, and gets it back with the last leg. Cancel any time, everything back. The page's own upkeep and the Node runner execute due legs; the page adds no fee.
